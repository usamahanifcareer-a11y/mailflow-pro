📧 Connect Your Gmail

💡 Why App Password? This lets you send emails from your own Gmail address without any Google verification. Free & unlimited.
  1. Go to Gmail → Settings → See all settings
  2. Navigate to Security → Enable 2-Step Verification
  3. Open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords
  4. App name: MailFlow Pro → Click Create
  5. Copy the 16-character password (remove spaces)
🔒 Your App Password is encrypted with AES-256-GCM
