Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

MailFlow Pro ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our email automation platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google, we collect:

  • Basic Profile Information: Your name, email address, and profile picture
  • Google Account Access: Encrypted OAuth tokens to send emails on your behalf
  • Email Content: Emails you compose and send through our platform
  • Recipient Data: Email addresses and company names you upload

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Send emails on your behalf via the Gmail API
  • Store your templates, signatures, and recipient lists
  • Provide AI-powered email writing assistance
  • Track email open rates for analytics

3. Gmail API Data Usage

MailFlow Pro's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We only access Gmail to send emails you compose
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your email data to third parties
  • We do not use your email data for advertising
  • We do not allow humans to read your emails

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely in Firebase Firestore (Google Cloud). Your OAuth tokens are encrypted using AES-256-GCM encryption before storage. We use HTTPS for all data transmission.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your data at any time.

6. Data Deletion

To delete your account and all associated data:

  • Email us at usama.hanif.career@gmail.com
  • We will permanently delete your data within 30 days
  • You can also revoke access from your Google Account permissions

7. Third-Party Services

We use these services to operate:

  • Google Gmail API — Send emails on your behalf
  • Google Drive API — Store attachments and logos
  • Firebase Firestore — Database and authentication
  • Vercel — Hosting infrastructure
  • AI Providers — Groq, Google Gemini, Mistral for AI features

8. Children's Privacy

MailFlow Pro is not intended for users under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
